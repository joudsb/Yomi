# Color Research — Reference Brand Palettes

Analysis of the five reference palettes collected on the Figma "Brand Kit" page (2026-07-06). For each color: what it represents, how it feels, and its personality.

## TikTok

**Turquoise #25F4EE** — The glow of a screen in the dark. Cold, electric, digital. Represents nightlife and restless youth energy. Feels futuristic, slightly synthetic. Personality: edgy, hyperactive, nocturnal.

**Red #FE2C55** — Pure heat and desire, the emotional opposite of the turquoise. Represents urgency, virality, the pull of the next video. Feels loud and impulsive. Personality: bold, provocative, attention-seeking.

**Black #000000** — The stage. Full-screen video darkness that makes the two neons vibrate. Represents immersion and endlessness. Personality: mysterious, cinematic.

*The system:* turquoise + red are deliberately mis-registered like a 3D/glitch print — the brand IS motion, sound, and sensory overload frozen into a logo.

## Snapchat

**Yellow #FFFC00** — Pure attention: sunshine, a camera flash, a Post-it. No other major app owned yellow, so it's unmissable on a home screen. Represents spontaneity and the present moment. Personality: cheeky, young, impulsive, ephemeral — bright like a flash, gone like a snap.

**Black #000000** — The anchor. Gives the ghost its outline and the yellow its legibility. Represents secrecy (disappearing messages). Personality: private, matter-of-fact.

## Google Meet

**Green #00AC47** — "Go." Presence, availability, the green light of a camera. Personality: positive, dependable, fresh.
**Dark Green #00832D** — Depth and maturity behind the go-signal. Personality: grounded, professional.
**Yellow #FFBA00** — Optimism and warmth, the human moment inside a work tool. Personality: friendly, approachable.
**Red #E94235** — Urgency and action: hang up, record, alert. Personality: assertive, decisive.
**Blue #2684FC** — Trust, clarity, communication. Personality: reliable, corporate, even-tempered.
**Dark Blue #0066DA** — Authority under the trust. Personality: serious, institutional.

*The system:* Google's four primaries say "simple, universal, helpful" — childlike accessibility carrying enterprise trust.

## Instagram (sunset gradient)

**Blue #405DE6** — Legacy anchor (the original Polaroid-blue app). Trust, familiarity. Personality: stable, nostalgic.
**Purple Blue #5851DB** — Twilight; the moment day turns expressive. Personality: transitional, curious.
**Purple #833AB4** — Imagination, individuality, rarity. Personality: creative, expressive, mystical.
**Purple Red #C13584** — Creativity meets emotion. Personality: charismatic, self-assured.
**Rose #E1306C** — The signature: hearts, likes, warmth, connection. Personality: flirty, social, emotional.
**Bright Red #FD1D1D** — Passion and liveness, "happening right now." Personality: dramatic, intense.
**Red Orange #F56040** — Sunset energy, travel, adventure. Personality: adventurous, warm-blooded.
**Orange #F77737** — Sociability and enthusiasm. Personality: outgoing, energetic.
**Orange Yellow #FCAF45** — Golden hour, the light every photo chases. Personality: sunny, hopeful.
**Light Yellow #FFDC80** — Dawn light, softness. Personality: gentle, dreamy.

*The system:* not one color but a spectrum — the full emotional range of moments people share. The gradient itself means diversity and motion.

## Google Pay

**Blue #307DEF** — Trust with money, the non-negotiable. Personality: dependable, unflappable.
**Green #2DA94F** — Money itself; "payment went through." Personality: reassuring, prosperous.
**Yellow #FDBD00** — The friendly highlight: rewards, offers. Personality: warm, generous.
**Red #EA4335** — Alerts and importance, used sparingly so it's obeyed. Personality: direct, protective.
**Gray #5F6368** — Quiet utility; recedes so your numbers are loudest. Personality: discreet, professional, invisible on purpose.

*The system:* same Google primaries as Meet but weighted to blue/gray — playfulness dialed down, custody of money dialed up.

## Takeaways for Yomi

Yomi sits between these worlds: the spontaneous camera culture of Snapchat/TikTok and the structured reliability of a task tool (Google's territory). The palette will need (1) one ownable, high-energy signature color for capture/celebration moments (the yellow/turquoise lesson: own a color nobody else owns), (2) a calm, trustworthy base for the task/planning layer (the blue/gray lesson), and (3) a semantic accent system for task states — done, pending, overdue (the Meet lesson: color as instant status language). Instagram's lesson: emotional range can live in one gradient if the app is about moments.
